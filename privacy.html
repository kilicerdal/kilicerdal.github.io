Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. About This Application

Recruiter AI Dashboard is a personal automation application used to organize recruitment opportunities, analyze job descriptions, and maintain a job-matching dashboard.

2. Information Accessed

The application uses Google Sheets authorization to read recruitment-related spreadsheet records and synchronize job-matching results.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to organize recruitment opportunities, classify job descriptions, calculate AI matching scores, and generate dashboards.

4. Data Storage and Processing

The application processes recruitment information locally using Python and Ollama AI. Relevant records and generated results may be stored in local files and authorized Google Sheets spreadsheets.

5. Data Sharing

The application is intended for personal use. Recruitment records are not sold or used for advertising. Google Sheets data is not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties.

6. Security

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to access permitted Google Sheets data. Access credentials are maintained separately from the public website.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Recruitment information may be retained in local files and authorized spreadsheets until manually deleted. Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
